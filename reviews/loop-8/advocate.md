# Advocate: loop 8, high tier

## Summary
Both critics land in the same place (66.0 and 66.5), and most of their big calls hold up. I concede that the impact
has no camera kick, the creek ford reads as blue paving, the horizon ring is one terraced decal wall on every course
with fogged "ghost" tiers, and the landscape team select is cramped. Critic A got several facts plainly wrong,
though, and three of its gaps rest on those errors:
- **Figures.** They do have part seams, split legs, separate boots and studded helmets.
- **Trees.** The crowns are studded wherever their tops face the camera.
- **HUD labels.** The labels measure 8 to 10 px in cap height, not 5 px.
- **The dusk pack on the phone.** It separates cleanly, rings included.
- **Water.** It does leave the tub on the hit.

Critic B undersells mobile against its own anchor and doubts team identity at chase distance. The captures show 11
distinct crews. Measurements below are in capture pixels: 1280×800 desktop, 780×1688 portrait, 1688×780 landscape,
and 480×300 per strip frame.

Proposed total: **69.0** (the critics' mean is 66.25).
