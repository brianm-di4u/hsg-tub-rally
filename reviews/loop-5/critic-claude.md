# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 5, high tier

## Verdict
This is a coherent toy town, and it's now clearly brick-built near the camera. Studded baseplate grass (`high/desktop/R05`, `R08`, `R11`), plate-checker roads, glossy figures with printed faces and uniforms, real bath water in the tubs (`crops/high/R02-preview-captain`) and a brick-panel UI (`high/desktop/R02`, `R18`) all hold up. The Row and the Arlington are recognisable. It still stops short of the `worlds-1` bar wherever the camera looks past about 30 m. The mountain backdrops and horizon ridges are flat stepped planes with no trees or studs (`R05`, `R06`, `R12`). The trees are bare slabs with no studs or bevels (`R09`, `R10`, `R11`), and the facades are brick textures on flat walls. Feel is the weakest area: the slide strip shows detached white clumps instead of a charging steam trail, there is no orange at "hot steam", and no water slosh can be seen in any strip. On phones the new HUD tiles collide in both orientations. Scores land mostly at 6.5–7. The authenticity gate (≥ 6) passes. Characters, UI and typography are at their gate of 7. Materials, scenery, lighting, animation, readability and mobile are below 7.

## Scores

**Characters: 7.0.** The figures read as original plastic toys. Faces are printed with real expression (the shouting captain and grinning crew in `crops/high/R02-preview`, angry brows on the Out-of-Towners in `crops/high/R04-kart_3`). Uniforms are printed with reflective bands, X-straps and a hydrant badge (`crops/high/R03-player-rear`, `crops/high/R02-preview-captain`). Helmets carry moulded studs (`crops/high/R15-player`, the left figure). The tubs are built from parts: a raised team-stripe tile, a rim ring, a dark base plate, gold claw feet and capped wheels (`crops/high/R02-preview`). Water now shows as a deep blue sheet, with the duck sitting in it (`crops/high/R02-preview-captain`). Teams stay distinct at chase distance: black "H" Velvet Mob (`high/desktop/R07`), navy Bubble Patrol (`R11`), tie-dye against orange (`R12`) and white Lather & Linen (`R13`). Against `drive-4` (9.5) the figures lack hair or rear head prints: the backs of heads are bare tan blocks (`crops/high/R03-player-rear`). The white interior wall of the tub still shows fine vertical streaks at 2.5× (the interior wall in `crops/high/R02-preview-captain`). Translucent grey smears sit on the road beside the wheels in static countdown close-ups: at (820,650) in `crops/high/R03-kart_5` the road reads luma 111 against 75–93 elsewhere. That puts this at the 7 anchor, not above it.

**Materials: 6.5.** The figures and tubs have convincing ABS sheen, with highlights that follow the helmet and rim curvature (`crops/high/R02-preview`, `crops/high/R15-player`). Near the camera the baseplate studs are shaded, and the 4×4 plates keep a tonal pattern further out (`high/desktop/R05` grass, `R08` bank). Window glass carries a sheen and a streak (`R05` facade). The road plates vary in shade. Against `worlds-1` (7), everything beyond the props is a texture or a flat colour. Facades are brick textures on flat planes, with no bevelled corners or studded ledges (`R05`, `R13`). Tree canopies are untextured slabs with hard edges and no studs or highlights (`R09`, `R10`). Goat Rock is a brick texture on angled blocks (`R09`). The ridges are flat colour (`R12`). No clear part reads as transparent plastic apart from the item orbs (`R10`).

**Scenery: 6.5.** The baseplate ground, tile roads, brick buildings, lamp posts, fences, awnings and crowds read as a brick town (`high/desktop/R03`, `R04`, `R14`). Each course has a different palette and set dressing: the switchback bank and retaining wall (`R08`), the terracotta summit road (`R10`), the novaculite-white rock cluster (`R09`) and the blue ford (`R19`). The new terraced banks are a real gain (`R08`, `R19`). Against `worlds-4` and `drive-1`, the backdrops break the illusion. Hot Springs Mountain and the outer ridges are flat dark-green or pale stepped walls with no trees, studs or plates (`R05`, `R06`, `R12`, where the ridge reads (177,194,183) flat against a sky of (185,213,227)). Repetition is obvious: identical awning runs and one conifer model everywhere (`R04`, `R09`, `R11`). The plaza (`R17`) and Bridge Street (`R13`) are stock brick blocks with no hero set piece.

**Lighting: 6.5.** There's a directional key light with soft-edged shadows (tree shadows in `R08` and `R19`), contact shadows under the tubs (`crops/high/R02-preview`, luma 23–60 under the base against ~89 open road), and haze that fades the ridges toward the sky colour (`R12`). Day, dusk and sunset each have their own palette. Dusk is the best of them: lamp pools on the walk, a pink gradient sky from (151,98,92) at the top to (195,126,98) low, and buildings silhouetted (`R07`). The Row facades separate from the sky (`R06`). Against `worlds-4` (7), sunset (`R11`) is the weak one: the sky is amber, but the road sits at about (49,37,34) and the trees at (22,33,19), with no warm key, rim or bounce on the figures. The flat backdrop planes take no light variation at all (`R05`, `R06`). Nothing approaches the photographed-toy look of `bj-1`..`bj-3`.

**Animation and feel: 6.0.** In `strips/high/R15` the crew visibly runs around the tub with rigid toy limbs: positions change every frame, the legs scissor, the blue splash beads burst around the tub in frames 3–7 and settle by frame 8. That part is convincing. In `strips/high/R16` the crew circles while the "Ring around the tub" bar fills from about 10% to about 95% across 8 frames, which reads well. `strips/high/R14` is the problem. The shot is labelled "charging hot steam", but the spray is two detached clumps of white spheres sitting on the road beside and behind the tub (frames 3–8), plus pale-blue beads in 7–8. I saw no orange tint and no stream from the wheels. A FOV kick or roll can't be identified in eight stills. No water slosh shows in any strip. Against `drive-3` (effects 8.5), boost and slide feedback is weak. This is just below the 7 anchor ("slides, boosts… read with particles and camera kick; water visibly sloshes").

**UI / HUD: 7.0.** On desktop the hierarchy is clear and the style is one family: lap and time on a bevelled slate tile top left, place top right, the item orb and coin count top centre, the gauge and checklist bottom left, the map bottom right (`high/desktop/R04`, `R17`). The menus are brick panels with stud rows and stud-topped orange buttons that belong in the world (`high/desktop/R01`, `R02`, `R18`). Against `drive-3` (8.5) there are three rough edges. The gauge tile is a translucent grey box without the dark outline and bevel the other tiles have (bottom left of `R04`). The glass item orb and the teal callout banners ("WARM STEAM", "HOT STEAM!" in `R19` and `R17`) are two further styles. The phone overlaps are scored under mobile.

**Typography: 7.0.** The two faces form a clear scale: a chunky pixel display face for the title, READY, place and lap/time, and a rounded sans for buttons, tables and labels (`high/desktop/R01`, `R03`, `R18`). The numerals on the tiles read at a glance ("8TH" and "0:07.00" in `R04`). The results table is clean (`R18`, `high/mobile/R18`). There are two small faults. The item count "x3" is about 8 px tall and clipped by the orb's edge (`high/mobile/R17`, zoomed). The checklist labels sit at roughly 10 px cap height on desktop (`R04`, bottom left). Against `drive-2` this is solid but not characterful enough for a 9.

**Readability: 6.5.** Road edges and the centre dashes are obvious (`R04`, `R12`, `R13`). The item orbs pop (`R10`, `R11`). The judge stop reads, with its booth and banner (`R16`). The player's marker triangle is always present. Against `drive-3`, two things hurt. The player's ring takes the team colour, so the Steam Rangers' tan ring, about (168,142,90), nearly vanishes on the sunlit tan path, about (177,125,81), in `high/desktop/R19`. In packs the rival crews merge into one mass of bodies where individual tubs can't be separated (`R07` centre, `R12` left of the player). At dusk the dark crews sit on a dark road (`R11`, road about (49,37,34)). Phone frames hold up (`high/mobile/R04`).

**Mobile: 6.5.** The thumb controls never cover the tub in portrait or landscape (`high/mobile/R04`, `high/landscape/R04`). Portrait team select is laid out with care (`high/mobile/R02`), and landscape team select now fits all 12 cards beside the details (`high/landscape/R02`). The 4× CPU phone proxy holds 241 fps minimum, and SwiftShader 55. But the HUD isn't framed on purpose on either orientation. In portrait the lap tile is drawn over the top of the water gauge, and the item orb overlaps the bottom of the place tile (`high/mobile/R04`, `high/mobile/R17`, zoomed). In landscape the minimap overlaps the place tile (`high/landscape/R04`, `high/landscape/R17`).

**Authenticity: 6.5.** The Row reads as the Row. The Fordyce has its patterned upper storeys, arched windows, red tile roof and teal skylight (`high/desktop/R05`). A domed bathhouse sits on Central (`R03`, `R14`). The Arlington closes the vista with its twin cupola towers and buff brick (`R06`). The real race shows through the water gallons with the 10-gallon line, the towel/bath/soap/loofah checklist, ducks, flags and the judge stop (`R04`, `R16`). Against the 7 anchor, the mountains don't read as the Ouachitas: they're bare green walls (`R05`, `R06`), and the forest is one repeated conifer (`R09`). Bridge Street (`R13`) and the plaza (`R17`) are generic, and the mountain courses would need their HUD map to be named.

## IP check
Clean for LEGO. The heads are rounded boxes rather than cylinders, the hands are round mitts rather than C-clips, and the torsos are straight boxes rather than trapezoids (`crops/high/R03-player-rear`, `crops/high/R15-player`). No LEGO name or logo shows anywhere. One non-LEGO note: the foreground spectator in `high/desktop/R13` (left edge, about x 0–140, y 320–500) has a square head, a brown hair cap, a cyan tee and dark trousers. That reads very close to Minecraft's default character, so recolour or vary that crowd palette.

## Ranked gaps
1. **G-backdrop-ridges-flat** (scenery, WS4, severity 4, effort M, gain +0.5 scenery / +0.5 authenticity). Hot Springs Mountain and the horizon rings are flat stepped planes (`R05`, `R06`, `R12`). Cover them with instanced low-LOD brick conifer crowns and studded plate tops, layered in 2–3 depth bands. Test: in `high/desktop/R05` and `R12` no run of uniform ridge colour is longer than 60 px horizontally, and at least 3 tree crowns show per 100 px of ridge.
2. **G-slide-steam-fx** (animation, WS3, severity 4, effort M, gain +0.5). The slide spray in `strips/high/R14` is detached white sphere clumps with no orange at hot tier. Emit a continuous puff trail from the rear wheel contacts, coloured by charge from white to orange, and add a burst plus streaks on release. Test: frames 5–8 of `strips/high/R14` show at least 300 px of orange-tinted particles touching the rear wheels.
3. **G-water-slosh-visible** (animation, WS2/WS3 → WS3, severity 3, effort M, gain +0.5). No strip shows water moving. Tilt the water sheet visibly under lateral g and spill rim beads during slides and fumbles. Test: in `strips/high/R14` the water plane tilts at least 8° relative to the rim in at least 2 frames.
4. **G-trees-no-brick-detail** (materials, WS5, severity 3, effort M, gain +0.5). The canopies are flat slabs (`R09`, `R10`, `R11`). Put studs on the canopy plate tops at LOD0/1, add a bevel or edge-highlight shader and vary the shade per plate. Test: near canopies in `high/desktop/R10` show stud highlights and lit edges.
5. **G-phone-hud-overlap** (mobile, WS6, severity 3, effort S, gain +0.5 mobile). Lap over gauge and orb over place in portrait; map over place in landscape (`high/mobile/R04`, `high/mobile/R17`, `high/landscape/R04`). Stack the tiles with fixed gaps and move the map clear of the place tile. Test: at 390×844 and 844×390, no two HUD tile bounding boxes intersect.
6. **G-player-ring-contrast** (readability, WS6, severity 3, effort S, gain +0.5). The team-coloured player ring is lost on similar ground (`R19`), and packs merge (`R07`, `R12`). Give the player ring a fixed dark outline and a white inner stroke, and give rival tubs a faint team-colour rim-light at mid range. Test: the ring has at least 3:1 luminance contrast against the adjacent ground in every frame.
7. **G-sunset-key-light** (lighting, WS1, severity 3, effort S, gain +0.5). In `R11` the sky is amber but the scene is dark grey-brown. Add a warm, low raking key, longer shadows, a sun-side rim light and some lift in the ambient sky term at sunset. Test: sunlit road luma at least 80 and lit-face R/B at least 1.3 in `high/desktop/R11`.
8. **G-facade-brick-geometry** (materials, WS4, severity 3, effort M, gain +0.5). The facades are brick textures on flat walls (`R05`, `R13`). Add bevelled corner quoins, studded storey ledges and a brick bevel normal map. Test: corners in `R05` and `R13` show a lit bevel highlight.
9. **G-course-hero-pieces** (authenticity, WS4, severity 2, effort M, gain +0.5). Bridge Street (`R13`) and the plaza (`R17`) are generic. Build one reference-matched hero set piece per course. Test: each course capture contains at least one unique landmark model.
10. **G-hud-style-unify** (ui, WS6, severity 2, effort S, gain +0.25). The gauge tile lacks the outline and bevel the other tiles have, and the orb and teal banners are separate styles (`R04`, `R17`, `R19`). Give the gauge the same slate bevel and outline, and restyle the banners as slate plates with studs.
11. **G-road-ghost-smear** (characters, WS1, severity 2, effort S, gain +0.25). Translucent grey smears sit beside the wheels at rest (`crops/high/R03-kart_5`, `crops/high/R04-player`). Gate the dust and spray emitters by speed and make sure the soft particles fade at rest. Test: road luma variance within ±10 next to the wheels in R03 close-ups.
12. **G-tub-interior-streaks** (characters, WS2, severity 2, effort S, gain +0.25). Fine vertical streaks on the tub's interior wall (`crops/high/R02-preview-captain`). Smooth the interior normals and re-bake AO at higher resolution.
