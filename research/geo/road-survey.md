OSM data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Elevations are USGS 3DEP bare-earth (public domain), sampled bilinearly every 5 m along each way from the finest tile that covers it.

**How to read it.** *Length* is the sum of the OSM way lengths (a dual carriageway counts twice). *Spacing* is the distance between consecutive OSM vertices: small mean/max means the curve is well sampled; a large max means a long straight chord (or a coarse TIGER import) that needs smoothing or extra control points before it becomes a race spline. *Max grade* is over a 25 m baseline from the DEM, bridges and tunnels excluded (the DEM is bare earth, so a bridge reads as a dip). *Components* counts pieces of the road that do not share an OSM node; *cycles* is the cyclomatic number of the road's own ways (>0 means the road alone contains a loop).
